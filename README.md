# Silkenty Wellness Showcase

PROJECT: Silkenty concept homepage, standalone shop-style site (new

project, starting fresh, not related to any previous build)

WHAT THIS IS: Silkenty is a wellness tea brand (5 blends: Detox Reboot,

Purify, Awakening, Rejuvenate, Evolve). This is a concept homepage to show

a design direction, not a full site build.

ASSETS ATTACHED:

- Silkenty logo (script wordmark)

- hero.jpg, use as inspiration for the hero section's mood and imagery

- flat-lay.jpg, this shows what the 5 actual products look like. Use it

  as a REFERENCE for what each product/tin looks like, do not crop or

  cut pieces directly out of this image for use as product photos, it

  doesn't hold up visually when cropped. Instead, create clean, well-composed

  product visuals for each of the 5 blends inspired by how they look in

  this photo.

COLOR: Keep this to two accent colors only, green and orange, these are

Silkenty's own existing brand colors. Don't introduce additional colors

per product (no pink, no purple, no rainbow effect across the 5 blends).

Background should stay light/neutral, let green and orange carry the

color energy consistently throughout the page.

STYLE: This should feel like a shop/e-commerce site, product-forward,

with proper product cards for each of the 5 blends (image, name, short

description). Bright, clean, and inviting.

SECTION STRUCTURE:

1. Hero

   Full-width, using hero.jpg as inspiration for mood and imagery.

   Silkenty logo prominent. Short headline and tagline introducing the

   brand. Clear CTA button.

2. Products

   All 5 blends shown as proper product cards (Detox Reboot, Purify,

   Awakening, Rejuvenate, Evolve), each with a clean product visual,

   name, and short one-line description. This is the main section of

   the page, give it the most visual weight.

3. Brand story

   A short section on what Silkenty is about, wellness, real ingredients,

   the ritual of tea. Keep it brief.

4. Closing CTA / footer

   Simple footer with Silkenty branding and a closing call to action.

You have creative freedom on how each section is composed within this

structure, just keep the two-color rule and the product-card approach

for the products section in mind. Build something that feels considered

and cohesive, not overly complex.
